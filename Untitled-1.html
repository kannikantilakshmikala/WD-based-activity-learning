Simple, Nutritious Ingredients

Build meals around a variety of nourishing foods.

🥑 🥦

Your Healthy Kitchen Checklist

Keep versatile ingredients ready to make nutritious meals easier throughout the week.

  • Whole grains: oats, brown rice, wholegrain bread
  • Protein foods: lentils, beans, eggs, tofu, yogurt
  • Colorful vegetables: spinach, carrots, broccoli
  • Fresh fruits: apples, bananas, berries, oranges
  • Healthy fats: nuts, seeds, olive oil, avocado
  • Flavor boosters: herbs, spices, lemon, garlic

Everyday Nutrition Tips

Small habits can help support a healthy lifestyle.

🥦

Eat a Variety of Foods

Include vegetables, fruits, whole grains, protein foods, and healthy fats across your meals.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day. Your needs can vary with activity, weather, and health.

🥗

Plan Your Meals

Prepare ingredients in advance to make home-cooked, balanced meals more convenient.

🌾

Choose Whole Grains

Try oats, brown rice, whole-wheat roti, or wholegrain bread for more dietary fiber.

🍎

Enjoy Whole Fruits

Choose a variety of fresh or frozen fruit as part of your meals and snacks.

🍽️

Listen to Your Hunger

Eat mindfully, notice your hunger and fullness cues, and choose portions that suit your needs.
